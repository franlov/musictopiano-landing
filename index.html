FAQ

Fair questions

Does it work with any song?

Any audio produces a complete, valid score. With piano recordings or clean mixes fidelity is at its highest (~95%); with very dense mixes the arrangement adapts the music to the piano.

How much can I try for free?

Free accounts convert the first 30 seconds of any song — enough to hear the quality and see the full score layout. Full songs are one credit each.

How long does a conversion take?

Between 3 and 8 minutes for a normal-length song. When it's done, the score is in your library, ready to play and download.

Can I use the scores I generate?

Yes, for study and personal use — the same as transcribing by ear. MusicToPiano is a study tool: your audio and your score are yours.

What exactly do I download?

A print-ready PDF (with the generation date and settings used), a MIDI faithful to the original tempo, and MusicXML to open in MuseScore, Sibelius or Finale.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in the browser, on your phone too. Upload, listen, play, download.
